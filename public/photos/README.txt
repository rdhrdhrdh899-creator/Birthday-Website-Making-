HOW TO ADD YOUR PERSONAL PHOTOGRAPHS:
=====================================
1. Put your 3 personal photos in this folder (`/public/photos/`):
   - photo01.jpg
   - photo02.jpg
   - photo03.jpg

2. In `/src/config/storyContent.ts`, change the `src` values to:
   - `/photos/photo01.jpg`
   - `/photos/photo02.jpg`
   - `/photos/photo03.jpg`

3. Adjust `focalPosition` if needed (e.g. "50% 30%" to focus on faces).
Everything else (cinematic zoom, text overlays, mobile responsiveness) will automatically adapt!
